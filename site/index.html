A crash-recovery lesson

A saved result
with a missing input

A producer signals “ready” before flushing its source. The consumer saves a manifest, then a crash loses the input it depends on. Move the flush before the signal and the failure disappears.

Recovery requires manifest ≤ source: a saved result must not name a newer version than the source that survived. Step through both orderings and inspect every disk state allowed after each instruction.

    The model

    Records are atomic integers that persist independently. Actors communicate through volatile signals, and the explorer checks crashes at instruction boundaries with at most two preemptions. Torn writes, filesystem metadata and physical storage devices are outside this model.

    Read the semantics, explorer, or download the generated data. For the underlying crash-consistency problem, see PMRace, DURINN and PerSeVerE.
