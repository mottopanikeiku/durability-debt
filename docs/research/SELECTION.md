# Selection decision: Durability Debt

Decision date: **2026-09-05**. Sole human author: **mottopanikeiku (alp)**. The integration owner selected one active project, **Durability Debt**, as the highest-upside **conditional** direction among six investigated families under the recovered constraints. This is an ordinal research judgment, **[INFERENCE]**, not a measured ranking, global optimum, significance guarantee or finding of novelty.

## What was selected—and what was not

> Can persistence-sensitive joint schedule/crash exploration expose reproducible cross-process recovery failures in unmodified local Linux workflows substantially more efficiently than strong one-trace and composed schedule/crash baselines?

The selected *research question* is broader than the delivered *reference artifact*. The latter is a fully executable finite whole-record model/oracle with controls and strong baselines, including an elementary extrema baseline. It is not a proposed new reduction. Its assumptions are fixed existing records, atomic whole-record writes, independent per-record persistence prefixes, honored record flush, volatile handoff, fail-stop, and explicitly declared recovery predicates. It supplies a reproducible falsification/reference surface, not ext4, SQLite, namespace, mmap, opaque-process or native POSIX testing coverage.

A future product-aware reduction, preservation proof and restricted native process/FUSE backend are **planned research obligations**, not completed components. The absence of these results is consequential: the independent crash challenge's verdict is **conditional, near-KILL**. The project must survive the strong composition before any flagship contribution claim is justified.

The initial unsafe/safe litmus mechanism is already known from persistent-memory and filesystem work. A finite demonstration can establish that its own assumptions and checker distinguish orders. It cannot establish novelty, field prevalence, native feasibility, scalability or practical significance. Scratch traversal counts included duplicates and are intentionally not reproduced here; canonical executable outputs are the result authority.

## Selection method

Six read-only candidate investigations covered authority/confidentiality, remote workflow recovery, semantic verification/evidence invalidation, supply-chain artifact integrity, evaluation identifiability, and local state systems. Each was required to identify a real problem, strongest exact predecessor and boring composition, explicit contract and counterexamples, accessible assets/license boundaries, cheap kill experiment, sole-author complete scope and meaningful negative outcome. Independent challenges examined evidence liveness and crash/concurrency. Their assessments were synthesized, not promoted into facts by consensus.

The comparison deliberately has no numerical scores. Candidate reports contained some informal scores and proposed empirical thresholds; they were not calibrated measurements and are not used to manufacture a total utility function. The order below is the integration decision's explanatory synthesis. Relative order among rejected families has no scientific meaning.

## Load-bearing evidence for the eliminations

The linked sources remain the authority; judgments about subsumption are **[INFERENCE]**. Verification depth and unresolved licenses are recorded in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

### Authority and remote recovery

[EffectBound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02866), directly accessed on the decision date, describes finite hidden-state control for truthful policy-relative closure, strategy/impossibility/no-verdict outputs and the last preventable provider frontier. It is a primary direct predecessor, not merely a vaguely adjacent cancellation paper. [VERA](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30091), [REVISE](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.00643), [Atomix](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14849) and [Resume Means Resume](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03836) are scout-reviewed neighbors; their specific scope and artifact maturity must be rechecked before a reopened mechanism claim. Classical [RIFL](https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/rifl.pdf) establishes why durable server participation matters to exactly-once RPC. Public but unlicensed/inaccessible artifact material does not restore conceptual novelty.

### Evidence liveness

The independent challenge gives a concrete reduction: verifier plus immutable executable claim/specification plus complete effective inputs forms an ordinary action key; unchanged key permits reuse, unknown support forces rerun. [Bazel's Action/Command protocol](https://github.com/bazelbuild/remote-apis/blob/main/build/bazel/remote/execution/v2/remote_execution.proto), [Rattle](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.12737), [Ekstazi](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~gligoric/papers/GligoricETAL15Ekstazi.pdf), [STARTS](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~legunsen/pubs/LegunsenETAL17STARTS.pdf), [iCoq](https://doi.org/10.1109/ASE.2017.8115630) and [Hermes's merged ledger](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/pull/52285) provide exact comparison points. Negative filesystem dependencies and generated-artifact input distinctions are not newly discovered exceptions. [Stored Is Not Supported](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02127), whose primary abstract was independently accessed, additionally covers typed provenance, temporal validity and conditional assertion boundaries in persistent agents. Different application domains leave empirical work possible, not an empty mechanism space.

[TEBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06125v1) supplies the counterpoint: old tests can keep passing while becoming semantically stale. Replay can establish observed outcome preservation, not whether a test still expresses intent. Its [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/iSEngLab/TEBench) reportedly declares CC-BY-4.0 for metadata/annotations; this corrects a blanket inference from a repository root lacking a license. Embedded projects retain their licenses. [Nebius trajectories](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nebius/SWE-agent-trajectories) are potential replay inputs, not proof of exactly reconstructible intermediate state or permission to redistribute all embedded material.

### Supply chain

The directly accessed [PEP 825 appendix](https://peps.python.org/pep-0825/appendix-metadata-consistency/) explicitly constrains only two variant metadata keys and discusses a prior scan of cross-wheel dependency metadata. Its own limitations prevent turning that scan into a proof that all cohorts are coherent. Conversely, evidence that a wheel differs does not establish harmful divergence: generated bindings, interpreter-specific shims and native specialization can be legitimate. [AVP](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18180), [No Snake Oil](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21888) and [LastPyMile](https://doi.org/10.1145/3468264.3468592) are close artifact/source/equivalence predecessors. [PEP 740](https://packaging.python.org/en/latest/specifications/index-hosted-attestations/) authenticates file-scoped statements, not an automatic complete family-origin guarantee. These are substantial exact-comparator, standards and prevalence risks, not superficial objections.

### Evaluation

The directly accessed [Bayesian archive audit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17005) already reconstructs public evaluation histories, defines observability boundaries and uses fixed failure gates to refuse stronger claims. [EEE](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14516), [OpenEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.03244) and [CAFE](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10380) further narrow ingestion, item-level release and attribution claims. Ordinary [partial identification](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/b97478) is not made new by benchmark terminology. A new census must identify a consequential fact beyond missing fields and cannot synthesize missing historical generations by rescoring retained outputs.

## Why choose the riskier systems direction?

**[INFERENCE]** Durability Debt was preferred because a specific schedule-conditioned recovery failure and a strong product baseline can be stated without paid inference or speculative semantic judgment. A failed separation is itself useful evidence that the proposed sophistication is unnecessary. The local Linux deployment boundary offers a plausible systems contribution if it can be instantiated honestly. The wheel alternative has easier static execution but may collapse to an exact comparator; the trajectory alternative has practical relevance but no surviving new liveness mechanism. The other three families have especially direct predecessor or impossibility collisions.

This is a tradeoff, not dominance: native replay/model fidelity may prove harder than artifact comparison or trajectory reconstruction. The ordinal choice may be wrong. The next researcher must be able to kill it without defending sunk work.

## Acceptance and stop discipline

[PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md) defines exact literature collisions and a repeatable refresh procedure. [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) and [BASELINES.md](BASELINES.md) own operational gates and budgets. The selection-level conditions are:

- **Reference artifact acceptance:** executable, finite, deterministic, with explicit assumptions, exhaustive oracle, strong baseline/control comparison and auditable unique-outcome accounting. This is baseline completion, not thesis acceptance.
- **Algorithmic continuation:** a candidate must preserve the declared bad-outcome language and separate from strong independent schedule/crash composition and applicable PerSeVerE-style observation pruning. An elementary extrema shortcut is a strong null, not the proposed scientific contribution.
- **Native continuation:** separately establish feasible schedule control, legal supported crash realization and reproducible semantic recovery failure in unmodified restricted process workflows. FUSE request gating alone is insufficient.
- **Practical continuation:** establish non-planted, consequential workload evidence and end-to-end efficiency under matched budgets/oracles. Do not count only ordinary one-trace missing-fsync bugs as joint-exploration discoveries.
- **Kill:** exact prior-art collision; no substantive gain beyond routine composition; unsound preservation; unsupported native behavior silently approximated; only synthetic/ordinary missing-fsync findings; or stronger conventional prevention renders the added machinery uneconomic. Preserve failures and revise the decision explicitly.

No Graph/Loop management practice, number of commits, name, documentation volume, initial litmus or publication action is evidence of research significance. This is a new project, not a rebrand of retired HeliosTune, BranchPilot or Unseen Loop. Those retired theses and repositories remain outside this project's work.
